In normal RAG, the model just retrieves once and gives you an answer. But in \textbf{Agentic RAG} [2], the model behaves like an agent (a smart assistant that can plan, reason, and act step-by-step).
\vspace{0.5em}

So, Agentic RAG = \textbf{RAG} + Reasoning + Decision-making + Iterative refinement
